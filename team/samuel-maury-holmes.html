Samuel Maury-Holmes

Founder and CEO of Zenith Economics. IMPLAN Certified Economist Professional (ICEP).

Samuel Maury-Holmes is an economist who founded Zenith Economics in 2024. He leads the firm's economic and fiscal impact studies, from attendee surveys for a single festival to a statewide analysis of 75 airports.

Before founding Zenith he was Practice Lead at Beacon Economics. Over his career he has led studies for clients including Meta, Port San Antonio, LACERA and Brightline West.

He is one of the 42 economists listed in IMPLAN's Certified Economist directory.
