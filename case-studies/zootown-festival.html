Zootown Music Festival economic impact study

Net new economic activity from Missoula's outdoor music festival, measured with an attendee survey.

A new festival and its visitors

Zootown Music Festival is a multi-day outdoor music festival in Missoula, Montana, first held in 2025. The Lumineers and The Chicks headlined the 2026 festival, which draws fans from across Montana and other states.

The organizers wanted to know what the festival was worth to Missoula and the surrounding region. That meant separating net new spending, brought in by visitors who would not otherwise have come, from spending by residents that would have happened anyway.

Zenith Economics designed and executed a primary research study combining attendee intercept surveys with input-output modeling. We found the festival generated approximately $9.1 million in net new economic output for the Missoula region in a single edition.

Methodology

The study used a two-phase approach:

  1. Attendee survey. Field staff intercepted attendees on-site to collect data on origin (zip code, state, country), trip purpose (festival-only versus combined-purpose travel), party size, length of stay, and spending across categories including lodging, dining, transportation, retail, and entertainment.
  2. IMPLAN modeling. Spending data from out-of-region attendees was modeled in IMPLAN using Missoula-region multipliers to capture direct, indirect, and induced effects on jobs, income, and output.

In-region attendees were left out of the net new impact calculation because their spending would otherwise have gone to other local businesses. The result is a lower figure than a gross-spending estimate, and one that sponsors and city officials can cite.

Findings and press coverage

The results were cited by Billboard and NBC Montana in coverage of the festival’s return for 2026. Beyond the headline $9.1 million figure, the study documented:

  • Visitor demographics, including the share of multi-night out-of-state travelers
  • Average daily spending by category and by attendee origin
  • Hotel-room-night demand attributable to the festival
  • Tax revenue contributions to state and local governments
  • Attendee satisfaction and likelihood of returning the following year

The organizers have used the results with sponsors, in grant applications and with the press.
