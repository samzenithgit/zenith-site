CCC Alumni Survey Gift Card Drawing: Official Rules

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. YOU DO NOT HAVE TO COMPLETE THE SURVEY TO ENTER. A PURCHASE OR PAYMENT WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCE OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED.

1. Sponsor and administrator. The CCC Alumni Survey Gift Card Drawing (the "Drawing") is sponsored by CCC Foundation, 5150 Sunrise Blvd., Suite E1, Fair Oaks, CA 95628 (the "Sponsor"), which provides the prizes. The Drawing is administered by Zenith Economics, LLC, 355 South Grand Avenue, Suite 2450, PMB 2220, Los Angeles, CA 90071 (the "Administrator"), which collects entries, selects winners and delivers prizes. The California Conservation Corps (CCC), a California state agency, is not a sponsor of the Drawing and does not fund the prizes.

2. Agreement to rules. By entering, you agree to these Official Rules and to the decisions of the Administrator, which are final on all matters relating to the Drawing.

3. Entry period. The Drawing begins at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time on Monday, October 26, 2026 and ends at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on Monday, November 9, 2026 (the "Entry Period"). Entries received after the Entry Period ends will not be accepted.

4. Eligibility. The Drawing is open to former CCC Corpsmembers who, at the time of entry, are 18 years of age or older and are U.S. citizens or legal residents living in the United States. Current employees and Corpsmembers of the California Conservation Corps are not eligible. Employees, officers and directors of the Sponsor and the Administrator, and their immediate family members and household members, are also not eligible. Entrants must confirm on the entry form, or in their free email entry, that they meet these requirements.

5. How to enter. There are two ways to enter, and both give the same chance of winning:

  1. Online form. Go to the entry form linked at the end of the CCC Alumni Survey (in English or Spanish), answer the eligibility questions, and submit your email address.
  2. Free entry by email. Send an email to sam@zenithecon.com with the subject line "CCC Alumni Drawing" and include your full name, your email address, and a statement that you meet the eligibility requirements in section 4. You do not need to complete the survey to enter this way.

Limit one entry per person, regardless of method. Entries made through automated means will be disqualified. If a person enters more than once, only their first entry counts.

6. Entries are kept separate from survey answers. The entry form is separate from the CCC Alumni Survey. Entry information is stored apart from survey responses and is not used to identify survey respondents or connect anyone to their answers. Entering the Drawing has no effect on how survey answers are used or reported.

7. Prizes. Five (5) prizes will be awarded. Each prize is one $100 Visa gift card. Approximate retail value: $100 per prize; $500 total. Gift cards are subject to the issuer's terms and conditions. Visa is not a sponsor of the Drawing. Prizes cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash. The Administrator may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if a listed prize is unavailable. Limit one prize per person.

8. Drawing and odds. On or about December 9, 2026, the Administrator will select five winners at random from all eligible entries received during the Entry Period. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received. If fewer than five eligible entries are received, every eligible entrant will win a prize.

9. Winner notification. The Administrator will contact potential winners by email by December 16, 2026. A potential winner must reply within seven (7) days to confirm eligibility and say where to send the gift card. Gift cards will be sent within 30 days after a winner confirms eligibility. If a potential winner does not reply in time, cannot be contacted or is found ineligible, or if a prize is returned as undeliverable, the prize will be forfeited and an alternate winner may be selected at random from the remaining eligible entries.

10. Taxes. Winners are responsible for any taxes on their prize. Winners may wish to consult a tax professional.

11. Privacy. Entry information will be used only to run the Drawing, contact winners and deliver prizes. It will not be added to any mailing list, sold or shared. All entry information will be deleted once the prizes have been delivered. Winners' names will not be published.

12. General conditions. The Administrator may disqualify any person who tampers with the entry process or violates these rules. If the Drawing is compromised by fraud, technical failure or any other cause beyond the Administrator's control, the Administrator may cancel or modify the Drawing and award prizes at random from eligible entries received before the problem arose. The Sponsor and Administrator are not responsible for lost, late, incomplete or misdirected entries, or for technical problems of any kind.

13. Release and limitation of liability. By entering, you release the Sponsor, the Administrator and the California Conservation Corps, and their officers, employees and agents, from any claims, losses or damages arising from the Drawing or from accepting or using a prize, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

14. Governing law. These Official Rules and the Drawing are governed by the laws of the State of California. Any dispute arising from the Drawing will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Los Angeles County, California. If any part of these Official Rules is found invalid, the rest remains in effect.

15. Questions and confirmation of winners. For questions about the Drawing, or to request confirmation that the prizes were awarded, email Samuel Maury-Holmes at Zenith Economics, sam@zenithecon.com, within 60 days after November 9, 2026.
