Zenith Economics firm facts

The basic facts about the firm in one place, for procurement teams, journalists and researchers.

Last updated October 25, 2026

Zenith Economics, LLC is an independent economic consulting firm based in Los Angeles. We produce economic impact, fiscal impact and social impact studies for public agencies, universities, ports, airports, pension systems, event organizers and companies across the United States. We quote fixed fees, deliver proposals within 48 hours of an inquiry, and do not accept sponsored research or work where we have a financial stake in the result.

Models and tools

Our primary platform is IMPLAN. The team also works with REMI, RIMS II, Lightcast, Chmura and Eora Global multi-regional input-output (MRIO) tables, and uses Stata, R and GIS for data work and mapping. We use multi-regional models when a study spans more than one geography, so that commuting and trade between regions are captured.

Fees and timelines

Every engagement is quoted as a fixed fee. Typical ranges from our practice:

Fee rangeTypical scope
$3,000 to $8,000A targeted attendee survey or a small fiscal impact memo for a single-county project with existing data
$8,000 to $25,000A standard single-organization study (one campus, facility or festival) with a designed report and basic primary research
$25,000 to $75,000A multi-site, multi-region or methodologically complex study with substantial primary research
$75,000 to $150,000+Statewide system studies, large multi-stakeholder engagements, or custom modeling with executive presentation

Most studies take 6 to 12 weeks from kickoff, depending on data availability. Expedited studies can be delivered in one to two weeks. Proposals with scope, timeline and fee are sent within 48 hours of an inquiry. The cost guide explains what drives each range.

Standard deliverables

  • Designed final report, usually 20 to 90 pages, with tables, maps and a methodology appendix
  • Executive summary of 2 to 6 pages for press and officials
  • Presentation deck of 15 to 25 slides
  • Excel workbook with all inputs and outputs
  • Presentation to the client's board or council

Independence

We do not accept sponsored research and do not publish work in which we have a financial interest. Every study names its authors and documents its model, inputs and geography in a methodology section, and we present and defend our results to boards, councils and the press.
