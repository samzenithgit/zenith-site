Museum & cultural institution economic impact studies

Our approach

Museums, performing arts centers and other cultural institutions draw visitors who spend money at local businesses. An economic impact study measures that visitor spending along with the institution's own operations, construction and educational programs.

We work with museums, galleries, performing arts organizations, and cultural districts on studies that support capital campaigns, grant applications, government funding requests, and community advocacy. Studies cover operations and visitor spending, plus construction for new buildings or expansions.

Results we report

Visitor spending

Off-site expenditures by museum visitors on dining, lodging, transportation, and retail.

Institutional operations

Employment, payroll, and procurement spending by the museum or cultural organization.

Construction impact

Jobs and output from building construction, renovations, and exhibit installations.

Educational programs

Economic activity supported by school visits, workshops, and community outreach.

Cultural tourism

Non-local visitation driven by the institution, representing net new spending for the region.

Tax revenue

Sales, lodging, income, and property tax contributions at local and state levels.
