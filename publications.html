Publications

Thought leadership and research from Zenith Economics.

Zenith publishes original research when client work raises a question worth its own report. We do not accept sponsored research or publish work in which we have a financial stake.

Each report is modeled in IMPLAN (or REMI or RIMS II where appropriate) with regional multipliers calibrated to the study geography, and every assumption is documented in a methodology appendix.

Our work has been cited by Billboard, NBC Montana, Texas Public Radio, the Joplin Globe, Urbanize LA, and in official reporting by the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts.

New reports are released when client engagements surface broader research questions worth a standalone treatment. If you have a question you think Zenith should study, tell us about it.
